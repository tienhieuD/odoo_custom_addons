Orphan Mail Router

Never lose an email that Odoo cannot place.

Emails that match no alias, no reply and no record are kept in an inbox instead of being refused or lost. The module suggests the right record, moves the email into its chatter in one click, and can do the same by itself with rules.

Live demo: demo-orphan.duotek.io.vn (sign in as demo_alice, password demo)

A customer writes to the catch-all, to an address that does not exist any more, or replies from another mailbox: Odoo cannot attach the email to anything, so it is refused or bounced and the message is gone. Orphan Mail Router keeps every such email (text, sender, headers and attachments) in an Orphan inbox. For each one it proposes up to three records, found from the reply headers, from a code in the subject and from the sender. One click posts the email in the chatter of the record, and the usual notifications follow.

The inbox of lost emails

Date, sender, subject, a preview of the text, the address that received it, the number of attachments and the state. Filter by status, alias, sender, date or text, and group by address, sender or week.

The three best records, one click away

Each suggestion says why it was chosen and how well it fits: replies to a message of this record, code S00001 in the subject, recent record of the sender. Route here posts the email in the chatter of that record.

The email arrives in the chatter

The email becomes a normal message of the record, with the original sender as author, the same Message-Id, the attachments and the date of the email. If the customer replies later, Odoo threads the answer on the record by itself.

Route many emails at once

Select emails in the list, choose the model and the record, and tick the options to take along the other new emails of the same senders or of the same threads. The window shows how many emails will move.

Rules that handle the routine

A rule has conditions on the sender, the subject and the address that received the email, and does one of two things: route to a chosen record, or ignore (spam, notices). Rules are tried in order, when the email arrives.

How many emails are lost, and where

A graph and a pivot of the orphan emails per week and per receiving address show which alias or mailbox needs a better setup.

Settings in one place

Switch the module on or off, choose the models an email can be routed to and searched in, add the sender as follower, send one daily digest to the managers, and choose how long handled emails are kept.

Each person sees the aliases assigned

A manager sees every orphan email, or only the emails of the aliases you assign. Useful when sales and support share the same server but not the same mail.

The view of a restricted manager

The same inbox for a person assigned to one alias: only that alias is listed.

What you get

An inbox for lost emails

Emails that Odoo cannot route are stored with their sender, recipients, text, headers and attachments. Duplicates (same Message-Id) are stored once.

Suggested records

Up to three records, scored from the reply headers, a code in the subject (matched with the name, reference or number of the records of the models you allow), the alias and the sender.

Route, route again, ignore

Post the email in the chatter of any record you may edit, many emails at once, move it again if it was a mistake, or ignore it and restore it later.

Rules

Sender, subject or receiving address contains a text: route to a record or ignore. They run when the email arrives.

Daily digest and report

One email a day to the managers instead of one per lost email, and a weekly graph per receiving address.

Access by alias

Group Orphan Mail Manager, an optional list of aliases for each manager, and routing only to records the user can edit.

Safe by design

The standard flow of Odoo is kept.

Nothing runs from an email

The text of every email is sanitised before it is stored and shown: scripts, event handlers and active content are removed.

Standard replies stay standard

Emails that Odoo can route are routed as before. The module only acts when no route exists, and if it cannot store an email it leaves the standard behaviour alone.

Permissions are respected

Routing needs write access to the target record, checked for the user who presses the button. Rules run with the rights of the system.

Easy to switch off

One switch in Settings gives the standard behaviour back. Uninstalling the module removes its data and nothing else.

Limitations

What to expect, so there are no surprises.

Only new emails

Emails that arrived before the installation were already refused and cannot be recovered.

The sender is no longer refused

When no route exists the gateway used to refuse the email. With the module on, it is accepted and kept. The standard bounce answer of the catch-all is still sent.

Suggestions use exact codes

A code in the subject must equal the name, reference or number of a record of an allowed model. Free text is not searched.

Inline images

Pictures embedded in the text of an email are stored as attachments and may not show inside the text after routing.

Followers are notified as usual

A routed email is a normal message of the record, so its followers receive the usual notification.

Requirements & Installation

The module depends only on the standard mail module. It needs no external library.

  • How it works

Install the module

1. Copy the dwo_mail_orphan_routing folder into an add-ons path of your server.
2. Restart Odoo and update the apps list.
3. Install Orphan Mail Router.

Set it up

Open Settings, then Orphan Mail. Choose the models an email can be routed to (a few common ones are pre-selected when they are installed), the daily digest and how long handled emails are kept. Administrators are managers; add other people to the group Orphan Mail Manager. The Orphan Mail app has the Inbox, Reporting and Configuration menus.

The module extends the incoming mail routing of the mail module. When Odoo finds no route for an email (no alias, no reply to a known message, no fallback model) or answers with a catch-all bounce, the email is stored as an orphan email and the usual flow ends there. If a rule matches, it is applied at once; otherwise the suggestions are computed. Routing posts the email with message_post on the target record.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it change how Odoo routes normal emails?

No. Replies, aliases and the fallback model work as before. Only an email that would have been refused or bounced is kept.

Which records can I route to?

The models chosen in Settings, as long as they have a chatter (a mail thread). The user needs write access to the record.

Can I route an email again?

Yes. The message already posted is moved to the new record; no second copy is created.

Who sees the orphan emails?

The group Orphan Mail Manager. By default a manager sees all of them; in Settings, Access by alias, you can limit a manager to some aliases.

Is the text of an email safe to display?

Yes. It is sanitised when it is stored: scripts and active content are removed.
