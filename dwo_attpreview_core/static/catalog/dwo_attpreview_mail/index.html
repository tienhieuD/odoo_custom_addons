Email, calendar and contact card previews

Open .eml, .ics and .vcf attachments in the Attachment Preview dialog.

  • Email messages: headers, body and the list of attached files.
  • Calendar files: the events with their dates, places and attendees.
  • Contact cards: the fields of the card.

Content from remote servers (pictures, tracking pixels) is blocked, and the message is cleaned before it is shown. The file is read on your server; nothing is sent elsewhere.
