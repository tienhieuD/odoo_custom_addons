Advanced Email Composer for Odoo

Real emails from the chatter, clearly apart from internal discussion.

A Send email button next to Send message opens an inline form with From, To, Cc, Bcc, subject, body, attachments and templates. The email goes to the people you choose and nobody else.

Send message in the chatter is a discussion: it can email every follower, one email each, with a notification layout. It is not a business letter. This module adds a separate Send email action that sends one genuine email to the To, Cc and Bcc you choose, with a clean layout and no notification to followers. It needs the free Mail BCC Support module.

What you get

Inline composer

The form opens right in the chatter, no popup. Cc and Bcc are small buttons next to To and appear only when you need them.

Address badges

Type to get suggestions from users, partners and the addresses you typed before, or enter any valid email. The same address may appear in To, Cc and Bcc.

Reply-all by default

Replies start with the right recipients and an RE: subject, and the previous email is quoted in the body, Outlook style.

Reply and Forward

Every email message has Reply by email and Forward by email buttons. Forward copies the attachments.

Templates

Apply a template to fill subject, body, From, To, Cc, Bcc and attachments, with placeholders computed on the open record.

Drafts that save themselves

Unfinished emails are saved automatically. Each person can keep several per record and reload one by clicking it in the list under the form.

Approval before sending

Ask one or more people to approve. They get an email, an on-screen notice and an activity, and can approve and send, reject or reassign.

Company-wide control

Make approval mandatory for the whole company in Settings. The server then refuses direct sending, not only the button.

Easy to tell apart

Official emails show an Email badge with From, To and Cc under the title, so they never get mixed up with messages and notes.

How to use it

From the chatter of any record.

1. Click Send email

The form opens in place of the chatter history. On a thread that already has emails, it starts as a reply to all.

2. Write your email

Pick the recipients, add Cc or Bcc if needed, apply a template, attach files. The draft is saved as you type.

3. Send now or ask for approval

The Send email button offers Send now or Request approval and send.

Requirements & Installation

The module depends on the standard mail module and on dwo_mail_bcc (Mail BCC Support, free). It needs no external Python library.

Install Mail BCC Support first or together with this module.

The composer is for internal users only; portal and public users cannot use it.

Install the modules

1. Copy the dwo_mail_bcc and dwo_mail_compose folders into an add-ons path of your Odoo server.

2. Restart Odoo, activate developer mode and click Update Apps List in the Apps menu.

3. Search for Advanced Email Composer and click Install. Mail BCC Support is installed with it.

Reload your browser page after installing so the new assets are loaded.

Send an email

Open a record, click Send email, fill in the recipients and the message, then use Send now. Use Advanced to add a Reply-To address.

Ask for approval

Choose Request approval and send, pick one or more approvers (yourself included) and add an optional note. Approvers find the request in Discuss > Email Approvals.

Any one approver is enough to send; any one rejection is enough to stop. The email is sent from your address with your rights.

Email Composer settings

Settings > General Settings > Email Composer has two options for each company:

Require approval: nobody can send directly, every email goes through approval.

Delete unfinished emails after (days): drafts and pending or rejected emails with no activity for this long are removed automatically. Default 180 days.

Good to know

An email needs at least one recipient in To; an email with only Cc or Bcc cannot be sent.

Delivery is not tracked per Cc or Bcc address.

Images inside the body are links to your Odoo server, so recipients see them only if the server is reachable from the internet. Use Attach to be sure.

Sending to several records at once, scheduling and preview are not available yet. The interface is in English.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is Send email different from Send message?

Send message is a discussion that can email every follower separately. Send email sends one real email to the To, Cc and Bcc you choose, with a minimal layout, and does not notify followers.

Will customer replies come back to the record?

Yes. The standard message identifiers are not changed, so a reply is routed by the mail gateway to the same record.

Which From addresses can I use?

Your own email, the company email and the default sending address of the alias domain.

Can I force every email through approval?

Yes. Turn on Require approval in Settings. Direct sending is then refused by the server.

Who can see an email that waits for approval?

The author, the current approvers, anyone who was an approver, and administrators. Only the author can edit it.

Is the sent email kept?

Yes. The outgoing email is kept under Technical > Emails so you can check its status, errors and content.

Does it require the free Mail BCC Support module?

Yes, it depends on it for the Bcc, and installs it with this module.
