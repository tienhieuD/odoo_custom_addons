For internal users only. Portal and public users cannot use the composer.

Install the modules

1. Copy the dwo_mail_bcc and dwo_mail_compose folders into an add-ons path of your Odoo server.

2. Restart Odoo, activate developer mode and click Update Apps List in the Apps menu.

3. Search for Advanced Email Composer and click Install. Mail BCC Support is installed with it.

Reload your browser page after installing so the new assets are loaded.

Send an email

Open a record, click Send email, fill in the recipients and the message, then use Send now. Use Advanced to add a Reply-To address.

Ask for approval

Choose Request approval and send, pick one or more approvers (yourself included) and add an optional note. Approvers find the request in Discuss > Email Approvals.

Any one approver is enough to send; any one rejection is enough to stop. The email is sent from your address with your rights.

Email Composer settings

Settings > Email Composer, for each company:

Approval flow: off, the Send email button sends right away and there is no approval. On, every email is approved first.

Allow approving your own emails (flow on): on by default. Off, you cannot choose yourself as approver.

Approvers (flow on): the people who can be chosen. Leave it empty and every internal user can approve.

Can send without approval (flow on): these people always see Send now. The administrator is in the list by default.

Delete unfinished emails after (days): 180 by default.

Good to know

An email needs at least one recipient in To; an email with only Cc or Bcc cannot be sent.

Delivery is not tracked per Cc or Bcc address.

Images inside the body are links to your Odoo server, so recipients see them only if the server is reachable from the internet. Use Attach to be sure.

Sending to several records at once, scheduling and preview are not available yet. The interface is in English.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference with Send message?

Send message can email each follower separately. Send email sends one real email to the To, Cc and Bcc you choose, and does not notify followers.

Do replies come back to the record?

Yes. The standard message identifiers are kept, so the mail gateway routes the answer to the same record.

Can I make approval mandatory?

Yes. Turn on Approval flow. The server then refuses to send right away, except for the people you list in Can send without approval.

Can I stop people from approving their own emails?

Yes. Turn off Allow approving your own emails. You can also limit the approvers to a list of people.

Which From can I use?

Your own email, the company email and the default sending address.

Does it need Mail BCC Support?

Yes, it is free and installed with this module.
