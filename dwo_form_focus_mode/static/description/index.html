Form Focus Mode

Wider forms, one click.

Order lines, long forms and wide tables need room. Pick a layout with three icons next to the pager, and the chatter stays one click away on the right.

On a large screen Odoo keeps the chatter beside the form and limits the width of the sheet, so wide order lines and many columns get squeezed. Form Focus Mode adds three small icon buttons, like the view switcher, right after the pager of every form. They choose the layout for all your forms. Nothing to configure.

What you get

Three layouts

Normal (the default): the form as Odoo shows it. Always hide: the chatter is gone and the sheet uses the full width. Auto chatter: the chatter is hidden and a bar on the right, with an arrow and the words Auto chatter, opens it when you need it.

A drawer, not a page change

A click on the bar opens the chatter as a drawer on the right over a dimmed background: messages, notes, activities, followers and attachments, without leaving the form. Click the dimmed background, the close button or press Esc and it closes.

As wide as you want

The drawer is at least as wide as the chatter of Odoo (530 px) and has a handle on its left edge: drag it to make it wider or narrower. The width is remembered.

Remembered on every form

The layout is one choice for all your form views: open another record or another model and it stays as you left it. It is kept in your browser.

Keyboard shortcut

Alt+Shift+F goes to the next layout (Ctrl+Shift+F on a Mac, where Odoo reads Ctrl as Alt). Each button has a tooltip that says what it does.

Safe for your data

It only changes how the form is laid out in the browser. No models, no fields, no menus, no data; it depends only on the standard web module. Forms in a dialog or on a phone are left alone.

Normal: the chatter beside the form

The default Odoo layout on a large screen. The three buttons sit right after the pager, like the view switcher; the one in use is lit.

Always hide: the whole width for the form

The chatter is out of the page and the lines and columns have room. This layout never shows the chatter, on any form.

Limitations

What to expect, so there are no surprises.

Remembered in the browser

The layout and the width of the drawer are stored in the browser (local storage), for all forms: they are not shared between devices or browsers, and a private window starts in the normal layout.

Forms in dialogs and on phones

A form in a dialog, and a form on a phone, keep their normal layout: there the form already has the whole screen.

Attachment panel and sheet chatter

The attachment preview panel that some forms show beside the sheet is not changed. A form that puts its chatter inside the sheet keeps it there.

Forms without a chatter

A form that has no chatter shows no bar in auto mode; the buttons still change the width of the sheet.

Requirements & Installation

The module depends only on the standard web module and needs no external Python library.

  • Install

Install the module

1. Copy the dwo_form_focus_mode folder into an add-ons path of your Odoo server.

2. Restart Odoo, activate the developer mode, update the apps list, and install Form Focus Mode.

3. Open any form view: the button is in the control panel. No settings, menus or data are added.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it change my data or add fields?

No. It only changes the layout of form views in the browser.

How do I get the chatter back in auto mode?

Click the bar on the right edge (the arrow and the words Auto chatter): the chatter opens as a drawer. Click outside it, the close button or press Esc to close it.

Can I change the width of the drawer?

Yes, drag the handle on its left edge. It starts as wide as the chatter of Odoo and the width is remembered.

Is the choice per form or for all forms?

For all your forms: open another record or model and the layout stays.

Which Odoo versions?

The same module is published for the recent Odoo releases, Community and Enterprise: pick your version in the version selector of this page.
