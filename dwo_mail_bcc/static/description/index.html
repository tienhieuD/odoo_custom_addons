Mail BCC Support for Odoo

Blind carbon copy, finally wired from the composer to the mail server.

Adds a Bcc field to the full composer, to mail templates and to outgoing emails. Bcc recipients receive the email; their addresses never appear in any header seen by the other recipients.

Odoo already knows how to send a Bcc at the lowest level, but nothing connects it to the screens people use: the composer has no Bcc box and an outgoing email has no Bcc field. This module connects those points without rewriting the sending layer, so it stays small and easy to upgrade. It depends only on the standard mail module.

What you get

Bcc in the composer

The full composer (Send message) has a Bcc box, for a single record or for mass mailing. It is not shown for Log note.

Bcc on mail templates

A template has its own Bcc field. Placeholders such as {{ object.user_id.email }} work and are computed for each record.

Truly blind

Bcc addresses are delivered to, but never written in the visible headers, so To and Cc recipients cannot see them.

Bcc on outgoing emails

The outgoing email (Technical > Emails) shows the Bcc, and the message log keeps it in the Recipients tab for internal users.

Sent once, not many times

Odoo sends one email per recipient. The Bcc address receives exactly one copy, not one per recipient.

For developers

message_post(outgoing_email_bcc="a@x.com, b@y.com") is accepted, and the composer exposes the field for other modules to build on.

How to use it

Nothing to configure.

1. Open the full composer

On a record, click Send message, then the expand button to open the full composer.

2. Fill in Bcc

Type one or more email addresses in the Bcc box, separated by commas.

3. Send

Recipients in To and Cc see a normal email. The Bcc recipients get the same email without being listed.

Requirements & Installation

The module depends only on the standard mail module and needs no external Python library.

A Bcc needs at least one other recipient (To, Cc or a partner). An email with only a Bcc is not sent.

Install the module

1. Copy the dwo_mail_bcc folder into an add-ons path of your Odoo server.

2. Restart Odoo, activate developer mode and click Update Apps List in the Apps menu.

3. Search for Mail BCC Support and click Install.

Send with a Bcc

Open the full composer from Send message and fill in the Bcc box. It also works for mass mailing from a list.

Bcc on a template

Open an email template and fill in Bcc. Placeholders are computed for each record, and the result is added to the Bcc typed by hand without duplicates.

A Bcc address that is already a visible recipient is dropped from the Bcc, so nobody receives two copies.

Good to know

The quick message box of the chatter has no Bcc box; only the full composer has one.

When several records are sent at once, the same Bcc applies to all of them.

If an outgoing email carrying a Bcc fails to send, the Bcc is delivered when that email is sent again.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the other recipients see the Bcc address?

No. The address is used only for delivery and is removed from the headers before the email leaves the server.

Does it replace the standard mail sending?

No. It adds the missing connections on top of the standard mail module and does not copy or rewrite the sending code.

Who can see the Bcc addresses in Odoo?

Only internal users. The field on the message is not readable by portal or public users.

Can I use it with the chatter quick box?

Not yet. Use the full composer, or call message_post with outgoing_email_bcc from code.

Is it free?

Yes. It is also the base of the Advanced Email Composer module, but works on its own.
