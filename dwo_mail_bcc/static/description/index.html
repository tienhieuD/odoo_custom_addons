Mail BCC Support for Odoo

A real Bcc in Odoo mail.

Add a Bcc to the composer, to mail templates and to outgoing emails. Other recipients never see it.

Odoo can send a Bcc, but no screen lets you type one. This module connects the missing parts and keeps the standard sending code untouched.

In short

In the composer

A Bcc box in the full composer and in mass mailing.

On templates

A Bcc field with placeholders, computed per record.

Truly blind

Delivered, but never written in the visible headers.

Sent once

The Bcc address gets one copy, not one per recipient.

Logged

Kept on the outgoing email and in the message log.

For developers

message_post(outgoing_email_bcc=...) is accepted.

Requirements & Installation

Needs only the standard mail module. No external library.

A Bcc needs at least one other recipient (To, Cc or a partner). An email with only a Bcc is not sent.

Install the module

1. Copy the dwo_mail_bcc folder into an add-ons path of your Odoo server.

2. Restart Odoo, activate developer mode and click Update Apps List in the Apps menu.

3. Search for Mail BCC Support and click Install.

Send with a Bcc

Open the full composer from Send message and fill in the Bcc box. It also works for mass mailing from a list.

Bcc on a template

Open an email template and fill in Bcc. Placeholders are computed for each record, and the result is added to the Bcc typed by hand without duplicates.

A Bcc address that is already a visible recipient is dropped from the Bcc, so nobody receives two copies.

Good to know

The quick message box of the chatter has no Bcc box; only the full composer has one.

When several records are sent at once, the same Bcc applies to all of them.

If an outgoing email carrying a Bcc fails to send, the Bcc is delivered when that email is sent again.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can other recipients see the Bcc?

No. The address is used for delivery only and removed from the headers.

Who can see the Bcc list in Odoo?

Internal users only.

Is it free?

Yes. It is also the base of the Advanced Email Composer.
