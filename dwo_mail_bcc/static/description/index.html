Email Cc Bcc Reply-To for Odoo

The three address fields Odoo's composer is missing.

Add Cc, a hidden Bcc and a Reply-To to the full composer, to templates and to outgoing emails.

Odoo can send a Cc and a Bcc, but no screen lets you type them when you send a message, and a Reply-To is only offered to mass mailing. This module connects the missing parts and keeps the standard sending code untouched.

In short

Cc

A Cc box in the composer. Everybody sees it, and it gets one copy.

Bcc

Delivered, but never written in the visible headers.

Reply-To

Choose where the answers go. Checked: one valid address.

On templates

A Bcc field with placeholders, computed per record.

Everywhere

Full composer, mass mailing, outgoing emails and the message log.

For developers

message_post(outgoing_email_cc=..., outgoing_email_bcc=...) is accepted.

1. In the composer

Cc, Bcc and Reply-To are in the full composer, next to the subject.

2. On a template

A template can carry them too: Cc, Bcc and Reply To, with placeholders computed for each record.

3. On the outgoing email

The email that left shows its Cc, Bcc and Reply-To.

4. In the message log

The message keeps the Cc and the Bcc, visible to internal users only.

Requirements & Installation

Needs only the standard mail module. No external library.

A Bcc or a Cc needs at least one other recipient (To or a partner). An email with only a Cc or a Bcc is not sent.

Install the module

1. Copy the dwo_mail_bcc folder into an add-ons path of your Odoo server.

2. Restart Odoo, activate developer mode and click Update Apps List in the Apps menu.

3. Search for Email Cc Bcc Reply-To and click Install.

Send with Cc, Bcc and Reply-To

Open the full composer from Send message and fill in Cc, Bcc and Reply-To. It also works for mass mailing from a list.

On a template

Open an email template (developer mode, tab Settings) and fill in Bcc and Reply To. Placeholders are computed for each record.

An address that is already a visible recipient is dropped from the Bcc, so nobody receives two copies.

Good to know

Reply-To takes one valid address. When you set it, the answers go to that address and not back to the record, as Odoo does for any forced Reply-To.

The Cc and the Bcc get one copy, not one per recipient. The quick message box of the chatter has no such boxes; only the full composer has.

If an outgoing email fails to send, its Cc and Bcc are delivered when that email is sent again.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can other recipients see the Bcc?

No. The address is used for delivery only and removed from the headers.

Where do the answers go with a Reply-To?

To the address you typed. As with any forced Reply-To in Odoo, the answers are not routed back to the record.

Who can see the Cc and Bcc lists in Odoo?

Internal users only.

Does it change the standard sending?

No. It adds the missing connections on top of the standard mail module.
