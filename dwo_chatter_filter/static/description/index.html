Chatter Quick Filter for Odoo

Stop scrolling through the whole chatter, and make the filters your team needs.

A Filter by: row of coloured chips above the messages. Six chips come ready; the administrator adds, renames, colours, orders or hides chips, and writes the filter of each one.

Install the module

1. Copy the dwo_chatter_filter folder into an add-ons path of your Odoo server.

2. Restart Odoo, activate developer mode and click Update Apps List in the Apps menu.

3. Search for Chatter Quick Filter and click Install.

Reload your browser page after installing so the new assets are loaded.

Filter the messages

Under Filter by:, click a chip to see its messages only. Click All to see everything again.

Type in the box beside the chips to search the messages: contents, subject, author and sender, any case. It goes with the chip you chose, and the counts follow. X or Esc clears it.

Edit the chips (administrators)

Click Edit after the last chip. The list shows the chips: drag a line by its handle to change the order, use the toggle to archive or bring back a chip. New makes a chip with its own colour.

Open a chip to change its name, its colour and its filter. The filter is the usual rule editor on the messages: contents, type, author, date, and more. Save, reload the page, and every chatter shows the change.

The chips that come with the module

Discussions: Send message. Emails: emails received and sent. Activities: the message left by a done activity. Notes: Log note. Tracking: changed fields. Other: automatic messages and the rest.

A chip with no message is greyed out. At most 15 chips are shown.

Good to know

It works in the chatter of a record, not in Discuss or the inbox.

One kind of chip at a time. The search matches the stored text of the message, which is HTML, so a word that is part of a tag name can match. Odoo's own magnifier of the chatter is separate: while you use it, the badges are hidden.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can edit the chips?

Administrators (Settings access). Everyone else sees the chips and uses them, without the Edit link.

What does the search box look into?

The contents, the subject, the name of the author and the sender email of the messages, in any case. It waits for a short pause in your typing, works together with the chip you chose, and the chip counts follow the text.

What can a chip filter on?

Any field of the messages: words in the contents, type, subtype, author, date, and the kind of message the module computes (discussion, email, activity, note, tracking, other).

What if I write a filter that does not work?

The form refuses to save it and says why. A chip that breaks later (a field of a removed module) shows no number and filters nothing; the other chips keep working.

In which chip is an email sent from the Email Composer?

In Emails. It is an outgoing email, even though it has the subtype of a discussion.

Does the Activities chip filter the planned activities?

No. It shows the messages left when an activity is marked as done. The planned activities stay in their own list above the chips.

Does Load more work when a chip is chosen?

Yes. The server returns only that chip's messages, so Load more goes on in the filtered list.

Is my choice kept?

No. Open the record again and all messages are shown, so nobody misses a message without knowing.

Does it work with other modules that change the chatter?

It adds one row above the messages and does not touch the messages themselves. It runs together with the Email Composer.
