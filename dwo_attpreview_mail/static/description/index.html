Secure Email Preview: EML, MSG, ICS, VCF

Read saved emails without leaving Odoo.

Open Outlook and standard emails in Odoo, then the files inside them, level after level.

Click a .msg or .eml file in the chatter or in a file field and read the email in Odoo: sender, recipients, date, body and attachments. Click an attachment and it opens on top: a PDF, a spreadsheet, or another email with its own attachments, level after level. Nothing is downloaded and nothing leaves your server. Works with the free Secure Attachment Preview module, installed automatically.

Key Features

Emails inside emails

Forwarded messages and their attachments open one level after another, each in its own window, without downloading anything.

Outlook and standard emails

.msg and .oft from Outlook, .eml and .emlx from other mail programs. Calendar invitations (.ics) and contact cards (.vcf) too.

Safe to open

Scripts are removed and remote pictures are downloaded by your Odoo server, so the sender never sees the IP address or browser of the user. Only real pictures are shown: an address that is a program or a script (.exe, .sh or a script address) is refused with a visible error. A link in the email asks for confirmation and opens in a new tab. The email is shown in an isolated frame, and Odoo access rights apply at every level.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I open the files attached to an email?

Yes, and the files attached to an email inside that email, level after level. PDF and pictures open with the free core module; Word, Excel and PowerPoint files with Secure Office Preview. Any file can be downloaded.

Is anything sent outside my Odoo server?

No. The email is read on your server and shown in your browser. Pictures linked from the internet are downloaded by your server, so your IP address and browser stay private.

Do I need Outlook or extra packages?

No. Everything needed is shipped inside the module.
